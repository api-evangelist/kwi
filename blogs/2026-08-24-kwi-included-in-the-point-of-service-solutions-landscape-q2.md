MELVILLE, NY — August 24, 2026 — KWI, the unified commerce and point-of-service platform built for specialty retailers, today announced its inclusion in The Forrester Point-of-Service Solutions Landscape, Q2 2026, an overview of vendors in the point-of-service market. The report is available on Forrester’s website for purchase and to Forrester subscribers at forrester.com/report/RES197089 . “For specialty retailers, the work of running a store doesn’t divide neatly into technology categories,” said Sam Kliger, CEO of KWI.
