Retailers are constantly searching for new ways to keep up with evolving customer expectations, and a “mobile point-of-sale” (mPOS) has become an essential solution for providing omnichannel shopping experiences today’s customers demand. With its versatile nature, it’s no surprise that mPOS is quickly becoming the new standard for in-store customer engagement. Retail consulting partners cited flexible POS solutions as a top concern for retailers in a 2022 report with 60% of retailers stating that upgrading their POS is a top priority.
