Operational Intelligence in Motion: What Modern Retail Leaders Require Retail leaders are expected to make faster decisions than ever before. They are accountable for store performance, staffing, inventory flow, and customer experience across dozens or hundreds of locations. Yet many still rely on reporting cycles that reflect what happened yesterday rather than what is happening now.
