Every holiday season brings new challenges. This year, many retailers are preparing for the latest impact to stores: customers who have already done much of their shopping research before they walk through the door. More shoppers are using AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity to compare products, research brands, and narrow their options before deciding where to shop.
