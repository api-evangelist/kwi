How Top Brands Are Turning Purchase History Into Revenue Your customers’ purchase history is already in your systems. The question is whether your marketing team can act on it. Every purchase in the store or online tells you something specific: what this customer bought, when, in which channel, at what price, and how that purchase fits into everything else they’ve bought from you.
