# KWI

> KWI is a retail technology company (founded 1985, Melville, New York) providing a
> cloud-based unified commerce platform for specialty and department retailers: iOS
> mobile point-of-sale, merchandising, order management, CRM, clienteling and loyalty,
> real-time analytics, and AI product image search, delivered as a SaaS managed service.

Important: KWI does not publish a public developer portal, API reference, OpenAPI or
other machine-readable specification, SDKs, CLI, MCP server, or sandbox. KWI describes
an "API layer" in its platform architecture used to export data to third-party systems
and channels (CRM, ERP, SRP, supply chain), plus packaged connectors such as Shopify
Connect and a Klaviyo connector, but access and documentation are delivered through
KWI's professional-services organization under customer contract. Do not assume or
invent endpoints, base URLs, authentication schemes, or SDK package names for KWI.

## Platform

- [Platform overview](https://www.kwi.com/platform/): Unified commerce platform components.
- [POS](https://www.kwi.com/pos/): iOS-based mobile point-of-sale.
- [Merchandising](https://www.kwi.com/platform/merchandising/): Inventory analytics, replenishment, pricing, promotions.
- [Order Management](https://www.kwi.com/platform/order-management/): Buy-anywhere, fulfill-from-anywhere order orchestration.
- [Loyalty](https://www.kwi.com/platform/loyalty/): Customer loyalty program management.
- [CRM](https://www.kwi.com/platform/crm/): Customer relationship management and clienteling.
- [Snapshot](https://www.kwi.com/platform/snapshot/): Real-time sales and business analytics application.
- [AI Product Image Search](https://www.kwi.com/platform/ai-product-image-search/): Visual product search.
- [APIs](https://www.kwi.com/platform/apis/): Marketing description of KWI's application and API layers. Not a developer reference.
